## Free tier

The engine packages are Apache-2.0 and free to use, including in commercial products.
There is no runtime license check in the free tier.

## Commercial tier

`@wicklib/pro` is published to npm under the Wicklib Commercial License. Installing it does
not grant a license; a signed license token is required at runtime.

- Tokens are Ed25519-signed and verified offline.
- The payload describes the plan, seats, domains, expiry, features, and key id.
- No usage data is sent anywhere to validate a license.
- Public keys are embedded by key id, so keys rotate without a release.

## What is paid

Orderflow, footprint and volume profile; multi-pane layouts and workspaces; premium
script functions; strategy and backtesting; alert runtime.

## What is free

The renderer, built-in indicators, the WickScript language and runtime, the editor, and
the extension API.

## Trademark

The Wicklib name and logo are not covered by the Apache-2.0 license.
