## Free engine

The engine packages are Apache-2.0 and free to use, including in commercial products. There is
no runtime license check in the free tier.

## Commercial analytics

`@wicklib/pro` is a separate commercial package. Installing it does not grant a license; a
signed license token is required at runtime.

Current commercial capabilities include:

- Volume profile.
- Trade-tick footprint and orderflow.
- L2 depth aggregation.
- Absorption detection.
- Price alerts.
- Strategy backtesting.
- Script-to-strategy adaptation.

## License verification

- Tokens are Ed25519-signed and verified offline.
- The payload carries features, expiry, and customer metadata.
- No telemetry is sent to validate a license.
- The public key ships with Pro; the signing private key never ships.
- A license can be application-level or issued per customer by your backend.

## What remains free

The renderer, series, panes, indicators, drawings, themes, export, the WickScript runtime,
free `ta.*` functions, and the editor are free packages.

## Trademark

The Wicklib name and logo are not covered by the Apache-2.0 license.
